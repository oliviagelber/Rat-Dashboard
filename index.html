Geographic view uses the dataset's latitude/longitude coordinates projected with the Esri Web Mercator convention (EPSG:3857). ZIP circles are centered on the average reported coordinates for each ZIP code. Individual pins are a representative sample of records with usable coordinates. This file includes an offline vector view and does not load external map tiles.
Data & methodology. The workbook contains 114,507 records dated January 2010–June 2018. “Cases closed” uses records whose Status is Closed. Average resolution time is the mean of Closed Date minus Created Date for closed records with valid, non-negative date differences. The trendline is an ordinary least-squares fit to observed monthly counts and is extended 12 months as a directional forecast, not a prediction of exact future counts. Four records with an unspecified borough are included in the all-borough summary but not in the five-borough comparison or geographic view. Map sample pins are a subset of coordinate-complete reports.
